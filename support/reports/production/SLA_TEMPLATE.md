# Service-level agreement template — requires legal, customer, and operations approval

**Not effective.** ZoracleFlux currently has no hosted endpoint, monitoring
service, on-call team, public uptime history, or SLA.

Complete only after Gate B:

* service description, regions, dependencies, maintenance windows;
* availability measurement point, exclusions, and monthly calculation;
* support severities, response/update/restoration targets and channels;
* data protection, security incident notice, backup/RPO/RTO commitments;
* service credits/remedies, caps, force majeure, and termination;
* customer responsibilities and named provider contacts;
* effective date, version, signatures, and evidence owner.

Never convert pilot health checks or local metrics into an availability promise.
