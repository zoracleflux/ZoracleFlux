## Supplier selection checks

* Counsel: verify license/jurisdiction, professional liability, conflicts, scope,
  privilege and written deliverables.
* CPA: verify independence, AICPA eligibility and report scope; contract
  confidentiality and customer-sharing rights.
* ISO body: verify accreditation and scope through the relevant national
  accreditation body/IAF directory; an ISO consultant is not a certifier.
* Hosting/monitoring: verify status history, data location, DPA, retention,
  export, incident notice, access logs, and independent measurement.

No amount is asserted because prices vary by jurisdiction, entity size, scope,
service architecture, evidence period and provider quote. Record each approved
PO/currency/tax and actual cash in the project's cost ledger; this bundle itself
adds $0.00 cash spend.

## Zero-cash parallel track before funding

1. Run the local bundle checks and freeze the RC3 hash.
2. Create a redacted intake packet from `EXTERNAL_HANDOFF_CHECKLIST.md`.
3. Apply concurrently to USPTO/eligible law-school or local pro bono clinics;
   ask explicitly whether privacy, contracts, or technology-commercial matters
   are in scope. Patent-only acceptance is not a privacy review.
4. Ask an SBA SBDC/SCORE or incubator mentor for referrals, but record that the
   mentor is not counsel and cannot approve documents.
5. Complete NIST CSF/Privacy Framework, CSA STAR Level 1 (if the eventual
   service is eligible), OpenSSF Best Practices/Scorecard and SLSA provenance
   work. Store versions, commit SHAs and outputs in the evidence index.
6. Use official EU SCC/CNIL guidance and community templates only as marked
   drafts; have the accepted clinic or later paid counsel answer role,
   jurisdiction, transfer, retention and liability questions.
7. Apply for conditional cloud credits and grants only after confirming entity,
   age, funding, billing, data-location and expiry terms. A credit award is
   not cash for counsel and must not be represented as one.

The zero-cash track can produce a better handoff and public engineering signals;
it cannot move G1-G6 in `EXTERNAL_HANDOFF_CHECKLIST.md` without the qualified
reviewer, independent auditor/accredited body, real service, or signed
customer contract named by that gate.
