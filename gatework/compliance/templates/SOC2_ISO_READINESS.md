# SOC 2 / ISO/IEC 27001 readiness — SELF-ASSESSMENT ONLY

This page does not claim SOC 2, ISO/IEC 27001 alignment, certification, or an
audit opinion. The frameworks and scope must be confirmed with the selected
independent provider.

## Common preparation

1. Form entity and define system boundary, services, data, regions, exclusions,
   dependencies and assertion period.
2. Approve governance, risk methodology, policy set, control owners and
   evidence index; operate controls consistently for a documented period.
3. Perform access, SDLC, vulnerability, backup/restore, logging, privacy/vendor,
   incident and continuity tests; record exceptions and remediation.
4. Obtain management assertion/system description review and preserve evidence
   chain. Never backfill or alter evidence.

## SOC 2

Select an independent CPA firm; choose Type I (design at a point in time) or
Type II (design and operating effectiveness over a period), and select Trust
Services Criteria applicable to the service. Agree confidentiality and report
sharing. Only the CPA firm's issued report may be called a SOC 2 report.

## ISO/IEC 27001

Buy/access the applicable standard; establish an ISMS (clauses 4-10), risk
assessment/treatment, Statement of Applicability, internal audit, management
review and corrective action. Select an accredited certification body for
Stage 1 and Stage 2 audits; only its certificate is certification evidence.
