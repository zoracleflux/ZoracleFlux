# Legitimate no-new-cash pathways (research snapshot: 2026-08-21)

**Availability and eligibility are not assumed.** Each program must confirm
jurisdiction, financial need, entity status, conflict checks, data handling,
scope and intake in writing. None makes ZoracleFlux legally approved,
certified, hosted, or SLA-backed.

## Pro bono and university legal routes

| Route | What it can legitimately provide | What it cannot establish | Official/credible intake |
|---|---|---|---|
| USPTO Patent Pro Bono | Volunteer patent attorney/agent for qualifying under-resourced inventors/small businesses; regional eligibility commonly includes income, patent knowledge and a describable invention | Privacy notice, DPA, terms, security review, or SOC 2/ISO | [USPTO program](https://www.uspto.gov/patents/basics/using-legal-services/pro-bono/patent-pro-bono-program) |
| USPTO Law School Clinic Certification | Supervised student help for patent/trademark matters; each clinic sets criteria | General commercial/privacy advice or assurance | [USPTO clinic program](https://www.uspto.gov/learning-and-resources/ip-policy/public-information-about-practitioners/law-school-clinic-1) |
| Stanford Entrepreneurship Clinic | Pro bono transactional help for eligible under-resourced early-stage startups/nonprofits | Independent auditor/certification; acceptance is not guaranteed | [Stanford clinic](https://law.stanford.edu/mills-legal-clinic/entrepreneurship-clinic/) |
| Harvard Law Entrepreneurship Project | Supervised legal research for qualifying Harvard/MIT-affiliated founders; not a substitute for transactional counsel | Counsel approval of all ZoracleFlux contracts or a certificate | [HLEP client program](https://clinics.law.harvard.edu/hlep/apply/) |
| Pro Bono Net / LawHelp / local bar clinics | Intake/referral to local small-business, nonprofit and entrepreneur clinics; scope and means testing vary | Nationwide representation or guaranteed technology/privacy expertise | [Pro Bono Net guide](https://www.probono.net/oppsguide/), [LawHelp](https://www.lawhelp.org/) |
| SBA SBDC/SCORE-style mentoring | Free business mentoring and referrals; useful for preparing an intake packet | Attorney-client advice, legal approval, audit, or certification | Verify current local provider and role before sharing confidential material |

**Application packet:** entity/formation status, founder/contact, jurisdiction,
plain-language product/data map, customer/processor role hypothesis, current
templates, revenue/funding/need evidence, conflicts list, and the exact
questions counsel must answer. Redact real personal data and secrets.

## Free or open resources (use as scaffolding, not approval)

| Resource | Classification | Safe use |
|---|---|---|
| [EUR-Lex Commission Decision 2021/915](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dec_impl/2021/915/oj/eng) | Official EU controller-processor clause text | Use only after counsel confirms GDPR scope, roles and permitted modifications |
| [EUR-Lex Commission Decision 2021/914](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dec_impl/2021/914/oj/eng) | Official EU international-transfer SCC text | Select the correct module and complete transfer assessment with counsel |
| [CNIL GDPR toolkit/PIA templates](https://www.cnil.fr/en/my-compliance-tools/gdpr-toolkit) | Official regulator guidance/tooling | Data-map and risk assessment; not a DPA generator or legal sign-off |
| [ICO accountability framework](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/accountability-and-governance/accountability-framework/) | Official regulator self-assessment guidance | UK-facing gap assessment; not a certificate or universal law |
| [OpenRegulatory GDPR templates](https://github.com/openregulatory/gdpr-templates) | Community open-source templates | Inspect license, provenance, version and adapt; counsel must approve |
| [oneDPA](https://www.onenda.org/products/onedpa) | Community standard template | Draft/negotiation aid only; verify current text and counsel |
| [OSI licenses](https://opensource.org/licenses) / [Apache policies](https://www.apache.org/foundation/policies/) | Open-source licensing/policy references | License and contributor-policy review; neither is privacy or DPA approval |

## Self-attestation, readiness and security signals

| Signal | Evidence it can produce | Explicit non-claim |
|---|---|---|
| NIST CSF 2.0 / Privacy Framework profile | Organization-owned profile, gaps, owners and improvement plan | Voluntary framework; no certificate or legal opinion |
| CSA STAR Level 1 self-assessment | Provider-published questionnaire/registry entry where eligible | Self-assertion; not SOC 2, ISO certification, or customer SLA |
| OpenSSF Scorecard | Automated repository security checks and score | Snapshot of selected OSS signals; not penetration test, legal review, or certification |
| OpenSSF Best Practices badge | Passing/Silver/Gold practice questionnaire badge | Project practice signal; not a security warranty or audit |
| SLSA provenance | Verifiable build provenance and stated level for artifacts | Build supply-chain evidence only; not service availability or legal approval |
| GitHub security features | Public-repository code/dependency/secret findings and workflow logs where plan supports them | GitHub settings and green checks do not prove absence of vulnerabilities |

## Credits, grants and donated infrastructure

| Program | Legitimate use | Why it is not legal/certification funding by itself | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| [AWS Activate](https://aws.amazon.com/startups/credits/) | Conditional AWS service credits | Credits pay eligible infrastructure, not counsel or CPA fees; terms/expiry apply | Apply only with entity/domain and verify current eligibility |
| [Google for Startups Cloud](https://startup.google.com/cloud/) | Conditional cloud credits; public website/company email and age requirements are stated | Cloud credit is not a legal review or certificate | Apply and retain approval/terms as evidence |
| [Microsoft for Startups](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/startups) | Conditional Azure/technical benefits | Credits/benefits are not auditor or attorney engagement | Apply; do not imply endorsement |
| [Cloudflare Startup Program](https://www.cloudflare.com/startups/) | Conditional network/developer/security services | Infrastructure credits do not create monitoring evidence or SLA | Apply and isolate pilot/customer data until DPA and security review |
| [NSF America's Seed Fund](https://seedfund.nsf.gov/) / [SBIR.gov](https://www.sbir.gov/) | Competitive government grant funding if awarded and allowable | Application is not an award; allowability, entity and reporting rules apply | Ask program officer/accountant whether legal assurance is allowable before budgeting |
| Local incubator/accelerator/VC partner perks | May include donated counsel hours, credits or audit discounts | Referral/perk is not guaranteed, independent, or approval | Obtain written benefit, conflicts and deliverables before relying on it |

**Zero-cash rule:** applications, self-assessments, local spreadsheets and
open-source tools remain `$0.00` incremental cash in this bundle. Do not use an
unawarded grant, unapproved credit, volunteer review, badge, or template as a
customer-facing legal/security claim.
